Inter is the typeface for body text, buttons, labels, and the A3P wordmark.
The Inter woff2 files are the Latin subset from Fontsource (Inter 5.2.8),
which distributes the Google Fonts build.
Inter is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Ciottolo is the owner's display face, used only for h1, h2, and h3.
Ciottolo.woff2 is self-hosted. It covers space, basic punctuation, digits,
A-Z, a-z, Italian accented vowels, and the apostrophe ’ .
A heading character outside that set falls back to Inter.

Source Serif Pro is used only inside the G3 badge, for the level and the agent name.
The files are the Latin subset from Fontsource (source-serif-pro 5.2.5), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Headings stay in Ciottolo. Body text stays in Inter.

The pages do not request a font host at runtime.
